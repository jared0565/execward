# 0.1.10 Threat table: scores and sign-off

Date: 2026-11-22. Status: signed off by the owner on 2026-11-22.

The PRD's threat table ("Threat model and defences") listed 19 threats with their
controls but no likelihood or impact. Each now has both. The same table is in the PRD
(revision 186), directly under the "Residual risk" paragraph.

## What the scores mean

- **Scope.** Phase 1 as planned: read-only GitHub tools, one owner, the owner's own
  repositories, with the controls in the PRD's threat table built and working.
- **Likelihood.** The chance, within a year, that the attack is tried and gets past its
  control.
- **Impact.** The worst realistic harm if it does.
- **Basis.** Judgments proposed by Claude and accepted by the owner. They are not
  measurements, and no outside reviewer has seen them. The paid human security review
  (task G.10) comes before real client data or writes.

## What to take from it

- **The two to watch now.** Supply chain (Medium, High) and prompt injection (High,
  Medium). Supply chain is the only threat scored above Low for likelihood with High
  impact. Prompt injection is the only one scored High for likelihood.
- **Low likelihood, High impact (six threats).** Tool poisoning, sandbox escape,
  unconfined server process, token misuse, local listener abuse and console takeover. Each
  depends on one control holding, so each needs a test that the control does hold. The
  adversarial suite (tasks 0.3.1 to 0.3.3) is where those tests go.
- **Most "Low" scores rested on a control that was not built when this was signed off.**
  The scores describe Phase 1 with its controls in place. Until a control exists and is
  tested, its threat is worse than scored. For the position after the controls were
  built, see docs/decisions/2.3.3-threat-rescore.md.
- **Writes change the picture.** Nine threats rise at Gate 2. The table is re-scored
  before Gate 2, as well as monthly and after any incident, as the PRD's Governance
  section requires.
