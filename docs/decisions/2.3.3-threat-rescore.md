## What to take from it

- **Twice now a review by another model family has found real gaps the author's own
  tests did not:** nine findings at Gate 1 and eleven at Gate 2. The scores assume the
  controls hold; the reviews show that some did not until they were looked at by
  someone else. That argues for a review of the Phase 3 code before the first write,
  and for the paid human review (G.10) not being put off.
- **The two to watch are unchanged:** supply chain (Medium, High) and prompt injection
  (High, Medium).
