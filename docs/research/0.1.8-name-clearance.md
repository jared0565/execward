## What this does not cover

- **The EU and international registers.** The EU's combined search (TMview) refused
  automated requests, and I did not search WIPO's database. A mark registered only there
  would not show up above.
- **Unregistered rights.** A business can have rights in a name it uses without
  registering it. The earlier screen found no product called ExecWard in a web search or on
  npm, PyPI, crates.io or GitHub; I did not repeat that screen today.
- **Similarity as a lawyer would judge it.** I searched spellings and prefixes. Whether a
  mark such as EXECWEB or EXEC is "confusingly similar" for software is a legal judgment.
  My reading is that the risk is low, because the live marks cover unrelated goods and
  services, but that is an opinion, not advice.
- **Registering ExecWard as a trade mark.** Not done and not decided. Worth considering
  before any public launch (Phase 2 or later).
- **Prices.** Domain prices in the question put to the owner were rough figures from
  memory, not quotes.
