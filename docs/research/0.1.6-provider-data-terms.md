# Task 0.1.6: Anthropic's and OpenAI's data-processing terms

Read 2026-11-22 from the providers' own pages, fetched by a web-reading agent whose quotes I
have not re-read myself. Long pages were cut off part-way and the rest summarised, so check
the live page before relying on any single clause. This is not legal advice.

## Result

Approval depends on how the provider is reached, not only on which provider it is.

| Route | Confidential data | Client personal data |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropic API, organisation account under the Commercial Terms | Approved | Approved once the conditions below are met |
| OpenAI API, business account under the Services Agreement | Approved | Approved once the conditions below are met |
| Claude Free, Pro or Max, including Claude Code signed in with one of those plans | Not approved | Not approved |
| ChatGPT Free, Plus or Pro, including Codex signed in with one of those plans | Not approved | Not approved |
| ChatGPT Business or Enterprise | Approved | Approved once the conditions below are met |

Conditions for client personal data, from task 0.1.7: a completed DPIA, a transfer risk
assessment, and a check of the provider's sub-processor list.

## Why

| Point | Anthropic, commercial | OpenAI, API and business |
|---|---|---|
| Training on your data | No by default. Content you send as feedback can be used. | No by default, unless you opt in. |
| Retention | Deleted within 30 days. Flagged content up to 2 years. | Abuse logs up to 30 days. |
| Zero retention | By approval through sales. Not available for Mythos-class and Fable models, which keep 30 days. | By approval, for listed endpoints only. |
| Data processing addendum | Incorporated into the Commercial Terms by reference. Anthropic is processor. | Incorporated into the Services Agreement by reference. OpenAI is processor. A signed form is also offered. |
| UK transfers | UK Addendum in Schedule 3. | Standard clauses as amended by the UK Addendum. |
| Sub-processor list | Published, but the page did not load for the agent. | Published and read. |
| Where data is processed | Not stated on the pages read. | US and Europe by project setting, by arrangement with sales. UK is storage only. |

The consumer plans fail for two reasons. They sit under consumer terms, so no data processing
addendum applies and the provider is not your processor. And training is either on by default
(ChatGPT Free, Plus, Pro) or governed by a setting you must turn off (Claude Free, Pro, Max),
with feedback and safety-flagged content used for training even after opting out.

## What this means for ExecWard

- **The MCP clients are the exposure.** ExecWard's results go back to Claude Code and Codex.
  If either is signed in with a personal plan, every result it reads is under consumer terms.
  FR-38 must therefore treat a client as approved or not by how it is signed in, and the
  default for an unknown client is not approved.
- **An API key alone does not prove commercial terms.** Anthropic's Consumer Terms say they
  cover an API key "when used by individuals". The owner should confirm the account is held as
  an organisation under the Commercial Terms.
- **The verification reviewer (FR-48, FR-51) must use an API route**, never a personal plan.
- **Neither provider forbids personal data.** Both addenda describe special-category data as
  not intended. Health data needs a separate agreement with either provider.
